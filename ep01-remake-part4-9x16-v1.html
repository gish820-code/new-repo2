PART 4
RAINY DAY · EPISODE 1 (4/4)
The hilltop was cold, crowded, and above the water.
That was all that mattered.
Town volunteers had a tarp up and a check-in table running.
The school had walked every kid up the hill. They'd practiced.
Drizzle counted what he'd done right.
One. Drop, cover, hold on. Two.
On the coast, the shaking is your warning. Walk uphill, now. Three.
Grab the go-bag, not your stuff.
And four. He'd brought snacks.
Then the radio spoke again.
RADIO"…Highway 101 bridge at Gull Creek has collapsed… coastal roads closed…"
The only road out was gone.
The whole coast had been hit at once. Help would come.
Just not today. Maybe not this week.
DRIZZLE"So… nobody's coming?"
Below them, the water was still rising. Night was coming.
And somewhere down in the dark, a light flickered on where no one should be.
