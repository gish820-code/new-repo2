## Line 1 — Off the trail (Frame 1)

**Delivery:** Calm, clear.

    You took one wrong turn off the trail. Now every tree looks the same — and the light is fading fast.

## Line 2 — Stop (Frame 2)

**Delivery:** Calm, clear.

    Don't wander. Stop, think, observe, plan. Most lost people are found close to where they went missing — so stay put.

## Line 3 — Shelter (Frame 3)

**Delivery:** Calm, clear.

    First: shelter. Nights get cold fast. Prop a branch on a stump, rib it with sticks, and pile on leaves — thicker than your arm is long. Then line the floor with leaves too.

## Line 4 — Water (Frame 4)

**Delivery:** Calm, clear.

    Next: water. Follow the land downhill to a stream. Bring it to a rolling boil before you drink — or you'll trade thirst for sickness.

## Line 5 — Fire (Frame 5)

**Delivery:** Calm, clear.

    Then fire. Gather tinder, kindling, and fuel — from pencil-thin to wrist-thick. It warms you, dries your clothes, and keeps you calm.

## Line 6 — Signal (Frame 6)

**Delivery:** Calm, clear.

    Make yourself easy to find. Three blasts on a whistle. Bright colors in a clearing. Smoke by day, fire by night.

## Line 7 — Food can wait (Frame 7)

**Delivery:** Calm, a touch wry on "It can wait."

    And food? It can wait. You can last weeks without it — so skip any berries or mushrooms you can't name.

## Line 8 — Stay put (Frame 8)

**Delivery:** Calm, clear.

    Stop. Shelter. Water. Fire. Signal. Stay calm, stay put — and let the rescuers come to you.
