PART 3
RAINY DAY · EPISODE 1 (3/4)
The evacuation trail was steep, muddy, and exactly where the signs said it would be.
Point four miles to the hilltop. On a normal day, ten minutes.
With aftershocks, longer.
Halfway up, Mags' knees gave out.
Drizzle gave her his shoulder. They kept moving.
RADIO"…tsunami warning for the entire Washington coast… move to high ground immediately…"
Official word, finally.
But by then, everyone who'd listened to the shaking was already climbing.
Drizzle looked back.
A gray line stretched across the whole horizon.
It wasn't a cloud.
Twenty-two minutes after the shaking stopped, the first wave hit Gull Harbor.
It took the dock. The ferry. Then Main Street.
Drizzle wanted to stop and watch. Mags didn't let him.
Tsunamis come in waves, for hours.
The first one is often not the biggest.
You don't go back down until officials say it's safe.
