# Rainy Day — style lock (v1)

**Decision (user, after reviewing Episode 1 v8):** the series sticks with the **Episode 1 v5 style**.

The canonical look is the one in `ep01-full-16x9-v5.html` / `renders/ep01/ep01-*-v5.mp4` and described in
`docs/series-bible-v2.md` → "Visual style":
- hand-drawn ink line art on stormy blue-gray grounds (`assets/style/rainy-day-style-v1.css` palette); the yellow slicker is
  the only saturated color;
- the original rigged stick figures (Drizzle in the slicker, Mags with the gray bun, glasses, cardigan and hand-crank
  radio), with the later rig versions' cast additions for Episode 2+ (Gord, Tess, extras);
- the voiced cast and caption system of Episode 1 v5 (narration / dialogue / lesson captions, Kalam + League Gothic).

Not canonical (kept as versioned experiments, unchanged): the Episode 1 remake (style B, Phase B) and Episode 1 v8
(reference-video look: color cartoon plates, slim figures, dark-gray icon cards, brick-wall vertical frame, Sterling
narration). Their renders, scripts and tools stay in the repo for reference; nothing is deleted.

Episode 1 of record: **v5**. New episodes are built in the v5 style, continuing from Episode 2 v3.
